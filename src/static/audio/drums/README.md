# Drum samples

One-shots for the generated music of the audio activities
(`src/content/audio-effects/bilder/moon_song.py`), mono, 44.1 kHz, 16 bit,
starting at the attack, peak −1 dBFS.

- kick, snare, rim, hat-closed, hat-open, crash, ride: »DRSKit« 2.1 by Lars
  Muldjord and Deva (DrumGizmo), CC BY 4.0,
  https://drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:drskit (the close microphones
  of one velocity layer at about 80–90 %, mixed and time-aligned)
- clap: »percussive group clap« by DCphotofilm, CC0,
  https://freesound.org/people/DCphotofilm/sounds/456800/
- clap-2: »Clap 01.wav« by totalcult, CC0,
  https://freesound.org/people/totalcult/sounds/388546/

A page that plays music made with them names »DRSKit« in its credit line.
