# Electronic drum samples

One-shots for the song of the autotune widget (src/content/audio-effects/
bilder/liftoff_song.py), all CC0, from Freesound (the high-quality previews),
mono, 44.1 kHz, 16 bit, starting at the attack, peak −1 dBFS.

- kick: »Dance kick one-shot« by Alastair_Pursloe, https://freesound.org/people/Alastair_Pursloe/sounds/128968/
- snare, snare-roll: »909 Sn 01a dry«, »909 Sn 02a dry« by sakebeats, https://freesound.org/people/sakebeats/sounds/176348/, …/176352/
- clap: »House Clap 1« by Akssu, https://freesound.org/people/Akssu/sounds/465803/
- clap-2: »clap 3« by nahmandub, https://freesound.org/people/nahmandub/sounds/131333/
- hat-closed, hat-open: »Classic_TR_909_HatClosed_JdXi_1«, »…HatOpen_JdXi_4« by GRD-music-, https://freesound.org/people/GRD-music-/sounds/414967/, …/414964/
- crash: »DR Cymbal 19« by deadrobotmusic, https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/591652/ (cut at 3 s)
- riser: »UPLIFTER WHITE NOISE REVERB« by SkyernAklea, https://freesound.org/people/SkyernAklea/sounds/620818/
- reverse-cymbal: »Reverse Crash Cymbal Projection« (account since removed), https://freesound.org/people/deleted_user_7146007/sounds/383903/
- perc: »Perc - Deep Shaker« by spaghettiSFX, https://freesound.org/people/spaghettiSFX/sounds/795302/
