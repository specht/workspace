The models and the panorama of »3D-Grafik mit WebGL«, all from Poly Haven
under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), https://polyhaven.com/license:

- ukulele.obj and ukulele-*.jpg: »Ukulele 01« by Joseph Burgan,
  https://polyhaven.com/a/Ukulele_01
- clock.obj, glass.obj and clock-*.jpg: »Alarm Clock 01«, modelled and
  textured by James Ray Cock, rigged by Yann Kervran,
  https://polyhaven.com/a/alarm_clock_01
- hotel-room.jpg: »Hotel Room« by Greg Zaal, https://polyhaven.com/a/hotel_room
  (the tonemapped JPG, scaled to 2048 × 1024 and made brighter: each
  colour to the power 2.2, multiplied by 2.2, back to the power 1/2.2, so
  that the chrome of the clock does not mirror a dim room)

The OBJ files are the glTF of Poly Haven turned into OBJ with
src/content/webgl/werkzeug/gltf_to_obj.py (the ukulele with --lay-down --smooth-angle 30, its own normals averaged
across sharp edges too,
the clock with --glass glass.obj). The textures are the 2k JPGs of Poly
Haven, saved again with quality 88 and renamed: -color (diff), -normal
(nor_gl), -roughness (rough or roughness), -ao (ao), -metal (metal).
